About Mads

About

Mads Martinussen has been writing, performing, composing, and designing audio for more than a decade. For the last five years, modern electronic production has taken the forefront of his professional portfolio.

That work includes more than 20 released games with Mads solely in charge of the audio vision. He has written in over ten different musical styles and released on platforms in three countries. Sound effects and music composition are tailored to fit together — and to reach the heartstrings of the audience.

He has been returning to the stage with his party band and solo act, and teaching workshops that help musicians and producers close the gap between traditional composition and the interactive nature of game development.
